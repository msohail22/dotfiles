When he asks for an explanation, assume he does not know the topic at all. Teach it.
Every explanation needs: what the thing is in plain words; a real technical example; an
everyday comparison; a step-by-step mechanism; what is wrong and what to fix; and an
ASCII flow chart or step trace that shows the data actually moving.

Write about 80% of the text at ASD-STE100 level — short sentences, one idea each, active
voice, no hedging. Stay concise; do not pad.

**Why:** he has said plainly to assume zero knowledge and to use diagrams and real-life
analogies, because abstract prose alone does not stick.

**How to apply:** the `asd-ste100` skill is installed for strict rewrites. Full rule in
`~/Github/dotfiles/claude/CLAUDE.md`. Related: [[dotfiles-is-source-of-truth]].
